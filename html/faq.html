Frequently Asked Questions

ParentPath is a website designed to help Massachusetts families find and understand useful government and community resources. It brings information into one place and helps users continue to the official source when they are ready to learn more or take the next step.

No. You can browse services, search for resources, and view resource information without an account. An account is only needed for personalized features such as saving resources and using the My ParentPath checklist.

You can search for a specific service on the Find Services page or browse resources by category. Each resource page gives you a simple overview of the program, who may find it useful, what it can help with, and a link to the official source for more information.

If you are signed in, you can use the Save Resource button on a resource page to bookmark it for later. Your saved resources will appear in My ParentPath so you can easily return to them without searching again.

The My ParentPath checklist is a simple way to keep track of tasks and resources that may be useful before and after having a baby. You can check off tasks as you complete them and add or remove items based on what is useful for you.

ParentPath focuses on programs, services, and organizations that serve families in Massachusetts. Some resources may have their own location or eligibility requirements, so you should check the linked official source for the most current details.

Yes. ParentPath uses Google Translate to allow users to translate the website into a variety of supported languages. Language options on external websites may vary depending on the agency or organization providing the resource.

We try to keep ParentPath's resource information accurate and useful, but program details and requirements can change. For the most current information, always check the official website linked on the resource page.

No. ParentPath helps you find and understand available resources, but it does not process applications or determine whether someone qualifies for a program. When you are ready to apply, ParentPath will direct you to the appropriate official website.

ParentPath is being developed as a student capstone project focused on making Massachusetts family resources easier to find and understand. It is not an official government website or a replacement for the agencies and organizations that provide these services.
