About ParentPath

Helping Massachusetts families find the resources and support they need.

Our Mission

To make it easier for expecting parents and families in Massachusetts to find trusted information about government programs and community resources, so they can spend less time searching and more time focusing on what matters most — their families.

Who We Serve

  • Expecting parents
  • Parents and caregivers
  • Guardians and family members
  • Adults seeking child and family resources in Massachusetts

What We Do

  • Help families discover Massachusetts resources
  • Explain programs and services in clear language
  • Connect users with trusted official sources
  • Let users save helpful resources for later
  • Help parents organize important tasks with a personal checklist

What We Don’t Do

  • We do not determine eligibility for any programs.
  • We do not process applications.
  • We are not an official government website.
  • Always verify current program details with the linked official source.

How Information Is Selected

We research and compile information from official Massachusetts government websites and trusted organizations. We also consider feedback from our community through surveys to better understand the needs of families across the state.
